How to play

Wire your way from one idea to another.

  1. Every word links to its five most related words. Tap one to hop to it.
  2. Reach the target in as few hops as you can. Min is the shortest possible route.
  3. Backing up is free, but every hop you make counts, dead ends included.
  4. Stuck? Hints help, at a small cost to your score.

Related means "shows up in the same contexts", so words can be linked by meaning, place, or pop culture. Think laterally! A new puzzle arrives every day at midnight.
